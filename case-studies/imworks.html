From identity
to execution.

IMWORKS is presented here as a brand, creative production and digital case — not as a generic gallery of finished visuals.

The available documentation describes a six-stage operating logic: Discover → Strategize → Design & Plan → Produce & Fabricate → Execute & Install → Deliver & Evaluate. The case is strongest when that logic is used to explain how creative decisions connect to execution.

01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Creative, production and digital work inside an integrated system.

My documented role is positioned across creative, production and digital work. The available evidence supports the operating method and the connection between strategy, design, production and execution; it does not support claiming sole ownership of every IMWORKS output.

02 / APPROACH

A production system, not isolated outputs.

Discover

Understand the brand, goals, audience and challenges.

Strategize

Turn insights into strategic ideas and creative concepts.

Design & Plan

Develop designs, detailed plans and proposals.

Produce & Fabricate

Produce with attention to standards and craftsmanship.

Execute & Install

Execute and install with precision.

Deliver & Evaluate

Deliver, evaluate and pursue lasting value.

03 / WHAT THE CASE PROVES

Creative thinking has to survive production.

The documented workflow supports positioning IMWORKS around the connection between brand understanding, strategic thinking, creative development and physical/digital execution. It is therefore more useful as proof of an integrated working method than as a claim of a specific business outcome.

04 / SELECTED WORK

Visual evidence across brand and environment.

The broader IMWORKS archive includes work associated with retail branding, store environment and other creative production contexts. Where an individual project's exact scope or Sobri's personal contribution is not sufficiently documented, the work should remain labeled as selected work rather than being presented as a fully attributed business case.

05 / EVIDENCE STANDARD

Show the work. Keep the claim proportional.

This page deliberately avoids invented client outcomes, revenue, audience figures, ROI or full-project ownership. A stronger future version can add project-specific briefs, deliverables, production records, final outputs and attribution evidence as those materials are consolidated.

06 / LEARNING

One idea, many expressions.

The strongest lesson from the documented IMWORKS method is that creative direction should operate as a system: an idea needs to survive design, production, installation, delivery and evaluation without losing its strategic purpose.
