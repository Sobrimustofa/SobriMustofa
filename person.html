04 / THE PERSON

How I
work.

Specific working principles, not personality labels.

01 / THINK

Structure before noise.

I start by breaking a brief into what must be solved: objective, audience, production reality, people, constraints and delivery.

02 / DECIDE

Make the call.

Production environments need clear decisions. I value practical direction, defined responsibilities and communication that keeps the work moving.

03 / LEAD

Calm under pressure.

My strongest leadership experience comes from live and field production, where coordination and quality control matter at the same time.

04 / DELIVER

Finish the handoff.

A creative idea is only useful when it can survive production, execution and delivery. I stay close to the work until the handoff is clear.

05 / PROFESSIONAL STANDARD

Structured.
Strategic.
Hands-on.
