This case study focuses on strategy, management preparation and expansion readiness.

Stabilize the
system first.

Albaasel Indonesia was handled by Sobri through NUSAKARA CREATIVE across digital marketing, social media management, content production, management advisory and brand direction.

The work expanded beyond content output into preparation for a new Cimahi branch, where the immediate need was to restore structure and create a system that could support expansion.

01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Connecting strategy, management preparation and execution.

My documented role through NUSAKARA CREATIVE covered digital marketing, social media management, content production, management advisory and brand direction, with the work extending into preparation for the Cimahi expansion.

02 / CONTEXT

A growth opportunity inside an unstable operating environment.

Following multiple management resignations, the documented condition included no dedicated marketing/content team, inconsistent previous content activity, weak SOP, unclear execution consequences and incomplete handover of older documents.

03 / DIAGNOSIS

The problem was bigger than social media.

The work treated the situation as an organizational and expansion-readiness problem rather than simply a content-production brief. The strategic sequence became Research → Diagnosis → Direction → System → Execution → Learning.

04 / CIMAHI OPPORTUNITY

Research turned local signals into an actionable direction.

Network

Conversations with more than 30 SSBs were documented in a tournament/networking context.

Market signal

Research indicated a local need for SSB in Cimahi and a pattern of parents travelling to Bandung for certain needs.

Pipeline

More than 28 prospective new students were identified in the recorded opportunity work.

Progress

Development progress was recorded at around 7%; this is opportunity/progress evidence, not a final business result.

05 / EXECUTION

Build the operating layer that makes expansion possible.

Management

Operational management preparation, organizational system and SOP direction.

Systems

Financial/system preparation and process structure.

Expansion

Home base and permit preparation for the Cimahi branch.

Growth layer

Marketing, social media, recruitment and direction for branch development.

06 / LEARNING

Creative output is stronger when the operating system can carry it.

The case demonstrates a broader professional role: connecting research, management preparation, digital communication and execution instead of treating each as an isolated service. The long-term direction recorded for the project was a system capable of operating more remotely while developing young-player talent within the Indonesian football ecosystem.
