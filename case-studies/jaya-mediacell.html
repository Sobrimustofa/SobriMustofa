Jaya MediaCell began operating on 1 August 2026. Sobri's work through NUSAKARA CREATIVE started on 22 August, with the digital presence still at an early stage.

The starting record on 20 August showed 7 Instagram followers and 0 TikTok followers. The initial direction was not simply to chase followers, but to establish awareness, content direction and a clearer communication path for the device-service business.

01 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Creative, digital and social work from direction to response path.

My documented role through NUSAKARA CREATIVE covered social media management, content direction, creative development, content production, digital presence development and the early response path toward official WhatsApp.

02 / DIAGNOSIS

From an empty digital footprint to a usable communication system.

Jaya MediaCell provides repair services for Android, iPhone, MacBook, laptop and computer, with a stronger emphasis requested around iPhone and MacBook. The work therefore combined brand introduction, audience-facing content and digital presence rather than treating social media as a feed of disconnected posts.

03 / STRATEGY

Brand introduction → awareness → content direction → digital presence → response.

The working strategy connected content to a practical communication path: attract attention, encourage interaction, create intent, then move interested audiences toward the official WhatsApp channel.

04 / EXECUTION

Content, social and audience communication.

Direction

Social media management, content direction and creative development.

Production

Content production and digital presence development.

Communication

Audience communication and early response handling from digital interaction toward official WhatsApp.

Observation

Performance observation used to inform subsequent content direction.

05 / EARLY EVIDENCE

Growth is visible — attribution stays disciplined.

The project record documents 639 Instagram followers, 1,299 TikTok followers and 20 leads moving from Instagram/TikTok toward WhatsApp. These are presented as early-stage project data, not as a universal benchmark or as proof that every change was caused by one activity.

06 / LEARNING

Content creates the opening. Operations complete the journey.

The work showed that the path from content to communication depends on continuity between digital attention and operational response. Sobri's role included the initial response path from DM toward official WhatsApp; response speed and quality on the operational side remained an important factor in reducing drop-off.
